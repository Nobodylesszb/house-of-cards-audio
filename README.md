## Selection rules

1. Identify all plausible Frank asides first, then keep only complete, classic, language-/rhetoric-/thinking-rich segments with reliable boundaries and transfer value. The `frank-aside` tag is not a processing shortcut: every identified aside is either selected or skipped with a reason; do not make every aside an audio item.
2. Accept ordinary scenes when the speech itself is worth learning: negotiation, persuasion, strategy, conflict, crisis response, or a clear high-value exchange.
3. Exclude recap, title/credit material, subtitle-team metadata, character names alone, low-value filler, and fragments whose start/end cannot be recovered reliably. Record skipped Frank candidates and the reason in `skipped.tsv` when that file is created.
4. A selected item must keep complete audio and bilingual subtitles, plus context/scene, rhetoric and language highlights, transferable chunks, memorization segments, and a 60-second or 2–3-minute transfer exercise. Record at least: episode, tier/tags, start/end/actual duration, audio path, subtitle path, scene context, language/rhetoric notes, chunks, memorization breaks, and transfer task. Do not quote large blocks in the index.
5. Use tiers: `A-Core` (highest-value teaching item), `B-Classic` (memorable complete scene), `Recognition` (recognize/useful but lighter), `Context` (needed scene context), and `Extended 20–60秒` (complete short item in that length band).
6. Route selected material to Daily/Extended by its actual complete duration; never pad or silently truncate. A Frank aside may carry both `frank-aside` and a tier. If dialogue interrupts an aside, keep continuous parts as separate items (`part-1`, `part-2`); never splice them together.

## Two flat-package layers

This project follows the shared [transcript-expression-extraction standard](https://github.com/Nobodylesszb/self-improve/blob/main/english-learning/learning-system/transcript-expression-extraction-standard.md#11-两套平铺包):

- **Original collection package**: keep the complete selected material by show and season for lookup, including high-value dialogue, `Frank-aside`, `narration`, `Context`, `Extended`, and `Recognition`. It is an archive, not a daily playlist. Use names such as `001-HOC-S01E01-frank-aside.mp3` and `004-HOC-S01E01-recognition.mp3`.
- **Two-stage workflow**: each show first produces its own season-complete curated library, including narration/long-form expressions and unified tags. As soon as one batch is complete, use it for an integrated theme course; do not wait for another show. Later shows only add incrementally or replace weaker material with better material—do not rebuild the course. In short: single-show materials are for complete collection; integrated theme courses are for actual learning.
- **Active weekly package**: create `W01-主题名称/` from the collection, optionally mixing shows; keep 20–30 files only, with 30 as a hard cap. Suggested mix: 8–10 `Core`, 4–6 `Context`, 3–5 `Extended`, 3–5 `Narration/Frank-aside/blog-reflection`, and 2–3 `Recognition`.
- Episode-level `Extended` is normally 20–60 seconds; a course package may select an existing continuous complete segment up to 30–90 seconds, never truncate, pad, or join non-contiguous time or character tracks. Naturally continuous multi-character `Context`/`Extended` is allowed. Every `Extended` needs a personal speaking task.
- Train each theme four times: understand context → shadow → pause and complete the next line → speak for 60 seconds from personal experience without audio. Keep one physical audio copy; weekly packages reference it, and only export copies when an upload platform requires a flat directory.

## Subtitle handling

Use the season/source match documented in `processing-status.md`. Verify at least start, middle, and end anchors against the MP3 before cutting. Remove subtitle-team credits, URLs, and other metadata cues; they are not dialogue. No audio is generated by this indexing pass.
