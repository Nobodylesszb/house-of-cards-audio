### 001 — People stack so well

- Time / scene: `00:00:06.340–00:00:09.840`; Freddy's barbecue, opening direct address.
- English: “You know what I like about people? They stack so well.”
- 中文： “你知道我喜欢人哪一点吗？会排队。”
- 修辞/语言：疑问引入后用极短 punchline 收束；`stack` 把人写成可排列的物件，立即建立 Frank 的冷峻观察。
- 可迁移语块：`You know what I like about X? ...`; `They ... so well.`
- 背诵分段：`You know what I like about people?` → `They stack so well.`
- 迁移训练：介绍一个人或团队的优点，先用问句吊起期待，再用一个具体动词给出反常但清晰的答案。

### 002 — Freddy doesn't pretend to change

- Time / scene: `00:00:27.370–00:00:34.540`; Freddy's barbecue, direct address.
- English: “In a town where everyone's so carefully reinventing themselves, what I like about Freddy is that he doesn't even pretend to change.”
- 中文： “这个城市的每个人都在精心地改头换面，而弗莱迪的可贵之处，就是他甚至不装作要改变。”
- 修辞/语言：`where ...` 铺设环境，再用 `what I like about ... is that ...` 转入评价；`reinventing` 与 `doesn't even pretend` 构成反差。
- 可迁移语块：`In a place where ...`; `what I like about X is that ...`; `doesn't even pretend to ...`
- 背诵分段：`In a town where everyone's ...` → `what I like about Freddy ...` → `he doesn't even pretend to change.`
- 迁移训练：比较一个环境里的普遍做法与某人的反常优点，使用 `where ... / what I like ... is that ...` 完成对比。

- Time / scene: `00:00:36.650–00:00:49.260`; Capitol routine, direct address.

- 修辞/语言：先用正式行政语体 `the week's agenda`，再以自我修正 `the wrong word` 拆穿场面，最后落到荒诞视觉意象；暗黑幽默限制了模仿用途。
- 可迁移语块：`Every Tuesday I ...`; `... is probably the wrong word.`; `They ... while I ...`

- 迁移训练：描述固定例会时，先给出中性流程，再用 `X is probably the wrong word` 纠正表面说法；练习时把暴力意象替换成安全的夸张比喻。
